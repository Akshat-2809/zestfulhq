Where it began

At 13, Muskaan found a word in the newspaper, ran to the dictionary, and then asked her sister:

“Why is there no tool for introspection?”

She ended the conversation with: “Someone should build something like that.” That memory became the first seed of Zestful. At first she called it Smile, the English meaning of her name. That one was already taken.
